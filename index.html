The Curator’s Press

The Curator’s Press is an independent publishing company founded in Fort Worth, Texas, by Dr. Dawn Youngblood in 2026. It publishes her works of history, archaeology, and ideas.

Dr. Youngblood is an archaeologist and a former senior editor at Harcourt Brace. She served as a curator of archives and special collections at university and governmental institutions for more than twenty years. Her fieldwork has spanned predynastic Egypt, the Karoo of South Africa, and the Paleo-Indian sites of the American Southwest.

A curator traces each object to its source, tests whether it is genuine, and tells the public only what the evidence can truly support. We do the same for our readers. Read the Curator’s Press Standard.
