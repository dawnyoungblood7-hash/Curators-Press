The Curator’s Press Standard

Our standards are straightforward:

  • Scholarly Rigor
  • Insiders, Not Spectators
  • Human Judgment
  • Exacting Care

Dr. Dawn Youngblood, founder of The Curator’s Press, served as a curator of archives and special collections at university and governmental institutions for more than twenty years. A curator traces each object to its source, tests whether it is genuine, and tells the public only what the evidence can truly support. We do the same for our readers.

Too many books now sound authoritative without being true. Text has never been cheaper to produce. A single search can inundate knowledge seekers with bad choices that waste their time and money while feeding them an unhealthy diet of repackaged misinformation. The Curator’s Press is founded on a simple conviction: readers deserve better.

Each of our books comes from an author who has lived with the material, tested its boundaries, and verified its foundations. When you open a volume from The Curator’s Press, you are holding the work of a scholar who stands behind every page.
