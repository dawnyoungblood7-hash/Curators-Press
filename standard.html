The Curator’s Press Standard

Dr. Dawn Youngblood, founder of The Curator’s Press, served as a curator of archives and special collections at university and governmental institutions for more than twenty years. A curator traces each object to its source, tests whether it is genuine, and tells the public only what the evidence can truly support. We do the same for our readers.

Too many books now sound authoritative without being true. Text has never been cheaper to produce, and a single search can bury a reader in titles that waste time and money and repackage misinformation as knowledge. The Curator’s Press rests on a simple conviction: readers deserve better.

  • Scholarly Rigor. Every claim traces to a source, and a contested date or a disputed reading appears as a dispute, not as settled fact.
  • Insiders, Not Spectators. Our authors write from years in the field and the archive, not from secondhand summaries.
  • Human Judgment. A scholar decides what goes on every page and answers for each word.
  • Exacting Care. Every book passes through editing, fact-checking, and proofreading under a former senior editor at Harcourt Brace.

When you open a volume from The Curator’s Press, you hold the work of a scholar who has lived with the material, tested its boundaries, verified its foundations, and stands behind every page.
